## Narrative rule

Keep six main sections and the amortized-state-reconstruction argument.
The main body must explain both why a scalar renewal line works in a
concentrated reconstruction phase and why changing restoration/state
semantics can change the decision more than price retuning. Appendices
present experiments as question/design/result/mechanism, not command dumps.
Whole-file/output-block studies and full-input-growth renewal studies use
different workloads and denominators; their point estimates are not pooled.

Earlier broad-prior illustrative models are superseded developmental work,
not additional empirical evidence. They should be identified as such rather
than promoted to equal-weight claims or recopied for completeness.

fine grids. New experimental appendices include all restoration policies,
